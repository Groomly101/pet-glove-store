Questions

How do I use it?

Put the glove on, then gently pet your pet from head to tail. Loose hair collects in the silicone tips. Shake it off or rinse the glove.

Is it safe for my pet's skin?

The glove is made for brushing and petting, not for medical treatment. If your pet has skin irritation, stop use and contact your veterinarian.

Can I wash it?
